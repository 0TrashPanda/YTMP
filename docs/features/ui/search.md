# Search

The search bar searches all enabled sources, and shows **suggestions while you type** ✅
(YTM's: the typed part plain, the rest bold; they update in place while typing). Like YTM,
typing only suggests: **Enter** or a suggestion searches and shows the results. ↑/↓ pick a
suggestion, Esc closes them. With an empty box it shows your **recent searches** (kept in
the browser, ✕ removes one). On **phones** the header has a search button that opens a
full-screen search page (back arrow, the box, suggestions as rows with ↖ to put one in the
box and keep typing); the phone's back button closes it.

**Recognize a song** ✅, like YouTube Music's song search: the microphone icon at the right
end of the search box listens to the music playing nearby and shows that exact song (the
recording, not a similar one). Its name, artist and cover show as soon as Shazam knows it,
usually 3.5–4.5 seconds after the tap; a moment later **Play**, **Play next** and **Add to
queue** come, once it's found on YouTube Music. It tries after 2.5 seconds of sound, and
again after 4, 6 and 10 when that wasn't enough. A song that isn't on YouTube Music shows
**Search for it**. It only listens after a tap, and stops when the answer is there or you
close it; the app asks Android for the microphone the first time. Your device makes Shazam's
fingerprint of the sound itself (the sound never leaves it), and the room's host asks Shazam
(unofficially, like yt-dlp for YouTube), so it works in every room, also one hosted by a
phone. Shazam knows recordings, not humming, and sped-up or remixed versions often not; it
also refuses for a while when asked very often (many people at once). In the app and in
browsers on `https://` (or the same computer).

It also accepts **links** ✅: pasting a YouTube Music or YouTube link opens what it points
at right away, without Enter: an album, playlist, artist or podcast opens its page; a song
(or video, or episode) shows as the result (*From the link*, tap = add to queue), with *Open
the playlist it's in* when the link plays it in one (not YouTube's endless mixes). A YTMP
room link opens that room. A link that's typed rather than pasted shows one row instead of
suggestions (*Open this album*, …) that Enter or a tap opens. Links don't go into the recent
searches.

Like YouTube Music, **chips** above the results pick what to search for ✅: **All**,
**Library** (only your own library, see below), **Songs**, **Videos**, **Albums**, **Artists**, **Community playlists** and **Featured
playlists** (YouTube Music's own). *All* shows the **top result** as a big card, then a few
of each kind, each with **More** (which picks that chip). The chip stays as picked for
the next search. Songs and videos play like songs (tap = add to queue); albums, artists and
playlists open their page. **Podcasts** and **Episodes** have chips too (see below);
profiles are left out.

**Your library in search** ✅ (signed in to YouTube Music): *All* has a section **From your
library** with what matches in your liked songs, playlists and podcasts, by title or artist;
capitals, accents and small typos don't matter. The rest of the results never wait for it.
Songs in it work like everywhere else in search; playlists and podcasts open their page.
*More* picks the Library chip, with every match. Your liked songs are read once, a part at
a time, and kept on the device; after that only the newest ones are looked at, when they're
older than *New suggestions after* (Settings → Home) or after you pull your Library down.

**The order of the sections** ✅ (*Settings → Search*, or *Change the order of these
sections* under the results of *All*): move sections up and down, *Defaults* puts them back. By
default: top result, songs, from your library, artists, albums, videos, then the rest. A
section the saved order doesn't know yet goes where the default order puts it.

**On the Library tab the search bar searches your library** ✅ (*Library tab searches your
library* in *Settings → Search*, on by default): it says *Search your library* and suggests
from your library, without asking YouTube Music. Enter or a suggestion shows the Library
chip's results, with *Search everywhere for "…"* under them to run the same search fully
(the other chips do the same). On other tabs it searches everything again. On phones with
only a search icon at the top, a field on the Library page opens the same search. With the
setting off the search bar works as on any other page, and the Library page has its own filter field.

## Search history & recently played

- **Search history** ✅: recent searches are shown when you open the search bar, and can
  be removed one by one.
- **Listening history** ✅: *Library → History* (with a YTMP account), see
  [history in the room](../music/history-and-recommendations.md#history-in-the-room).
- **Recently played** (planned): a list of songs, albums, playlists and rooms you played
  recently.
