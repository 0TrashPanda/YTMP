# Sharing rooms and songs

How you hand a room or a song to a friend: with its link (the share sheet, a QR code for a
room), or by **holding two phones together**. For joining by room code, QR code or rooms
nearby, see [joining.md](joining.md).

## Holding phones together ✅

In the Android app, while a room's or a song's **Share** sheet is open, holding another phone
against yours hands over its link, like tapping an NFC sticker. The sheet says *Hold a phone
against this one*; with NFC switched off on your phone it offers to switch it on instead.

- **Only while the Share sheet is open**, and only while the app is on screen. Nothing goes
  out by accident, and a friend whose YTMP is open too doesn't "share back".
- **The other phone** opens the link like a scanned QR code:
  - a **room**: in the YTMP app when it's installed (straight in, no browser in between),
    otherwise in the browser, which can join the room too;
  - a **song**: YTMP's song screen when the app is installed (see below), otherwise the song in
    YouTube Music (app or website).
- **iPhones** (XS and newer) read it too, without an app: a notification opens the link (the
  room in Safari, the song in YouTube Music).
- Phones without NFC install and work as before; they just can't share or get things this
  way (the Share sheet doesn't mention it there).

## Sharing a song ✅

**Share** in a song's menu (⋮, right-click or hold, also the player's ⋮) opens its Share sheet:
the song, *Hold a phone against this one*, **Send…** (the phone's share sheet: a chat, mail,
…) and **Copy link**. Music that's only on your device (your own files) can't be shared.

A shared song's link is its **YouTube Music link** (`https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=…`), so
anyone can open it, with or without YTMP.

## A song shared with you ✅

When another phone hands you a song, or you share a song to YTMP from another app (YouTube
Music's *Share → YTMP*), YTMP shows it on its **song screen**, like YouTube Music's: the art,
title, artist and album, with

- **Play now**, **Play next**, **Add to queue**,
- **Start radio**,
- **Like** and **Save to playlist** (with YouTube Music signed in),
- **Open in YouTube Music** and **Share**.

In a room it acts on that room (only what your role there allows). With no room open, the
playing choices first ask **where**: a **new solo room**, one of the rooms on your phone, or a
room nearby on the same Wi-Fi.

## Settings → Sharing ✅

In the app:

- **Share by holding phones together**: on by default; off means the phone never answers as
  an NFC tag.
- **Open shared songs in YTMP**: on by default; off sends a song from another phone on to
  YouTube Music, as on a phone without YTMP (*Share → YTMP* always opens it here).
- **Shared songs show as**: a **full page** (default) or a **sheet** over the room, like the
  song menu.
- *Share* in the song menu can be hidden or moved like any action there (*Settings → Song
  menu*).
